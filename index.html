Getting It Done is a reentry navigation program. We help people returning home get identification, housing, employment, benefits, and healthcare — with one clear plan and one step at a time.

What We Do

We do not do everything. We do one thing well: we navigate. We organize the process, find the right providers, and stay with you until the connection actually happens.

Identification & Documents

Photo ID, birth certificate, Social Security card — the documents everything else depends on.

Housing Connections

We identify providers, make the call with you, and follow up until the connection is confirmed.

Employment Readiness

Resume building, applications, training programs, and interview preparation.

Benefits & Healthcare

SNAP, Medicaid, primary care, dental, and behavioral health connections.

Skills & Technology

Email setup, online applications, and the digital basics that jobs and benefits require.

Weekly Support

Every week during your first 30 days, we check in: what got done, what is blocking you, and what comes next.

How It Works

Eight clear steps. You always know what is happening, what comes first, and who is responsible.

Intake

A short conversation about your situation and what you need.

Safety & Scope

We make sure we are the right fit. If not, we connect you to the right resource.

Assessment

A full review: housing, documents, employment, health, benefits, and goals.

Priorities

We sort everything into what comes first — then pick your top three.

30-Day Plan

Specific tasks with owners and deadlines. You own the plan; we help you run it.

Execute & Connect

We work the plan together and connect you to vetted local providers.

Weekly Check-Ins

Five questions, every week, so nothing stalls quietly.

30-Day Review

Continue, transition toward independence, or hand off to the right provider.

Honest About What We Are Not

We do not provide:

  • Legal advice or attorney services
  • Medical, psychiatric, or clinical treatment
  • Emergency or crisis response
  • Housing itself — we connect you to housing providers

What we do instead:

  • Connect you to the right professional, the right resource, and the right program
  • Follow up until the connection actually happens — a referral given is not a referral done

Community Partners

Do you work with people coming home? We accept referrals from courts, probation, shelters, treatment programs, faith communities, and families. A warm handoff is always better than a phone number.
